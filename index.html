Marks Upload (Excel)

Download your class Excel sheet, enter the marks that have been reviewed, and upload it. You can upload the sheet several times as marking continues. Blank cells are skipped, so unfinished subjects can be completed later.

Scoring: Class Score is out of 50. Examination Mark is entered out of 100 and is automatically converted to 50.
